NON-DISCLOSURE & CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT
For Students Enrolled in Any Program Offered by the School of Internships (SOI)
This Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement (“Agreement” or “NDA”) is entered into between InEra Software Private Limited, operating through its School of Internships (SOI) division (hereinafter referred to as the “Company”), and any individual (hereinafter referred to as the “Student”) who registers for or participates in any program, course, workshop, or learning engagement offered by SOI via inerasoftware.com. This Agreement is executed in accordance with the Indian Contract Act, 1872 and the Companies Act, 2013.
By checking the acceptance box during registration, the Student agrees to be bound by the terms of this Agreement.
1. Purpose
The purpose of this Agreement is to protect the proprietary information, intellectual assets, business strategies, technical resources, and program materials of InEra Software Private Limited and its SOI division, which may be disclosed to or accessed by the Student during the course of their learning engagement.
2. Definition of Confidential Information
For the purposes of this Agreement, “Confidential Information” shall include, but not be limited to:
•	Artificial intelligence models, prompts, automation workflows, datasets, source code, algorithms, technical documentation, and software tools shared during the program.
•	Business plans, operational strategies, pricing structures, marketing approaches, partner details, and internal processes of InEra Software Private Limited.
•	Program curriculum, course frameworks, session content, recorded materials, SOI methodologies, and proprietary learning designs.
•	Client, partner, or internal communications shared in any form during or in connection with the Student’s association with SOI.
•	Any information disclosed verbally, electronically, visually, or in writing, whether before or after the execution of this Agreement.
3. Non-Disclosure Obligations
The Student agrees that they shall:
•	Not disclose, share, publish, transmit, forward, post, or reveal any Confidential Information to any third party, including through social media, public forums, or digital platforms.
•	Not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the approved learning activities within the SOI program.
•	Take all reasonable measures to maintain the confidentiality and security of information received during the program.
•	Promptly notify the Company upon becoming aware of any unauthorized disclosure, access, or breach of Confidential Information.
These obligations apply during the entire period of the Student’s enrollment and continue after the conclusion, withdrawal, or termination of such enrollment.
4. Intellectual Property Rights
•	All work products, projects, submissions, innovations, designs, software outputs, or developments created by the Student in connection with or during program activities shall be considered the sole and exclusive intellectual property of InEra Software Private Limited.
•	The Student shall not claim ownership, authorship, license, or commercial usage rights over any such work without the prior express written authorization of the Company.
•	The Student irrevocably assigns all rights, title, and interest in such work to the Company upon creation.
5. Restrictions on Use
The Student shall not:
•	Use Confidential Information for personal commercial gain, freelance engagement, or any external project without prior written consent from the Company.
•	Reproduce, adapt, reverse-engineer, share, or repurpose any program material, tools, frameworks, or strategies outside the SOI learning environment.
•	Apply, derive from, or adapt Company methodologies, AI tools, or curriculum content for use in competing programs, platforms, or institutions.
6. Consequences of Breach
In the event of any breach or threatened breach of this Agreement by the Student:
•	The Company shall have the right to initiate appropriate legal proceedings under applicable Indian law, including civil and criminal remedies.
•	The Company may seek injunctions to prevent further disclosure, as well as damages, compensation, and full recovery of losses sustained.
•	Such breach shall result in immediate termination of the Student’s enrollment in the SOI program, without any obligation to refund fees paid.
7. Duration of Confidentiality Obligations
•	This Agreement shall remain in force throughout the entire period of the Student’s enrollment in any SOI program.
•	Confidentiality obligations shall survive and continue to remain fully valid and binding for a period of two (2) years following the completion, withdrawal, or termination of the Student’s association with SOI, irrespective of the reason.
8. No Employment or Compensation Assurance
This Agreement does not create an employer-employee relationship between the Student and InEra Software Private Limited. Nothing in this Agreement constitutes a guarantee of employment, stipend, salary, placement, or future compensation. Any such arrangement, if applicable, shall be communicated through a separate written agreement.
9. Severability
If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, unlawful, or unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, such provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it enforceable. The remaining provisions of this Agreement shall continue in full force and effect.
10. Governing Law and Jurisdiction
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India, including the Indian Contract Act, 1872. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall first be attempted to be resolved through mutual discussion. If unresolved within thirty (30) days, the matter shall be referred to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts at Raichur, Karnataka.
11. Acceptance
By checking the acceptance box during registration on the SOI portal at inerasoftware.com, the Student confirms that they have read, understood, and unconditionally agree to comply with all terms of this Non-Disclosure and Confidentiality Agreement.
