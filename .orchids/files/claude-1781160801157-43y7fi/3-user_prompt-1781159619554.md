PRIVACY POLICY
How We Collect, Use, Store, and Protect Your Personal Information
This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) describes how InEra Software Private Limited (“the Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), operating the School of Internships (SOI) through its Website at inerasoftware.com (“the Website”), collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects the personal information of students, visitors, and users (“you” or “the User”) who register on or interact with the Website. This Policy is formulated in compliance with applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Indian Contract Act, 1872.
By registering on the Website or using our services, you consent to the collection and use of your information as described in this Policy.
1. Information We Collect
We may collect the following categories of information when you register or interact with the SOI program on our Website:
a. Personal Identification Information
•	Full name, contact number, and email address
•	Residential address, city, and state
•	Educational background and current institution details
b. Program-Related Information
•	Selected program or course and enrollment status
•	Registration and participation records
•	Attendance, assessment, and performance data
c. Technical and Usage Data
•	IP address, browser type, operating system, and device information
•	Pages visited, navigation patterns, and session duration on the Website
•	Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 6)
d. Payment Information
•	Transaction reference numbers and payment confirmation status
•	Payment processing is handled by authorized third-party gateways; the Company does not store full card or bank account details
2. Purpose of Data Collection
We collect and use your personal information for the following purposes:
•	To process your registration and manage your enrollment in SOI programs.
•	To communicate program schedules, updates, session access details, and notices.
•	To evaluate attendance, performance, and eligibility for program certificates or credentials.
•	To improve program quality, Website functionality, and the overall learning experience.
•	To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and audit requirements.
•	To send program-related communications and updates. You may opt out of non-essential communications at any time.
3. Sharing of Information
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Information may be shared only in the following limited and lawful circumstances:
•	With authorized SOI mentors, facilitators, and administrative staff strictly for program delivery and coordination.
•	With third-party service providers (such as payment processors or communication platforms) solely for program operations, under confidentiality obligations.
•	With institutional partners, only where a valid formal agreement exists and only to the extent necessary for program administration.
•	With law enforcement agencies, regulators, or government authorities, where required or mandated under applicable Indian law.
4. Data Retention
•	Your personal data will be retained for as long as you are an active participant in any SOI program.
•	Following the conclusion of your program, data may be retained for a reasonable period as required for certification, verification, and legal compliance purposes.
•	You may request deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the details provided in Section 9, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.
5. Data Security
We adopt reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure. These include:
•	Secure HTTPS connections on the Website.
•	Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel only.
•	Periodic review of data handling and security practices.
However, no digital system can guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to safeguard your registration credentials and notify us immediately if you suspect any unauthorized access to your account.
6. Cookies Policy
Our Website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience. The types of cookies we use include:
•	Essential Cookies: Necessary for basic Website functionality and secure login.
•	Analytics Cookies: Used to understand how visitors interact with the Website and to improve its performance.
•	Preference Cookies: Used to remember your settings and choices across sessions.
You may disable cookies through your browser settings at any time; however, doing so may affect the availability of certain Website features. Continued use of the Website constitutes consent to our use of cookies as described above.
7. Your Rights as a User
As a registered user, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:
•	Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
•	Right to Correction: Request correction of any inaccurate or outdated personal information.
•	Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.
•	Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for marketing or non-essential communications at any time.
To exercise any of the above rights, please contact us as described in Section 9.
